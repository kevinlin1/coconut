Vish--Anonymous course materials feedback and changes system



How it works:



This could be a system for students to give anonymous feedback to instructors on syllabi, course content, and the course in general in real-time. Students can leave a comment on any course page or document, for example the syllabus. This can generate a new syllabus with changes highlighted to that section. If the user doesn't like the changes, they can edit the changes, reject the changes, or reject and edit. Once they are done, they can submit the comment with the generated changes to the syllabus, which the instructor can see. The student can also disable the AI generation fully. The instructor can approve the change, reject the change, or edit. They can also reply to the student's comment and ask for further clarification.



Students will be anonymous by default but can be un-anonymous optionally. Students can be verified by Canvas LMS integration, or by signing in with their school email.



The UI for changes could look similar to the Google Docs suggestion system.

The changes made would be to the typst documents.





Benefits:

This could be more useful in many cases than the end-of-year course feedback system, because students can give feedback at any point in the course. The anonymity is also helpful for students who may prefer it when discussing sensitive information, which standard emails don't really offer.
